<h1>Gearage Rebranding Project</h1>

<p>
  For this project, I chose <strong>Gearage</strong>, a small outdoor consignment
  store located in Fort Collins, Colorado. I chose Gearage because I have worked
  there quite a bit and wanted to create something that felt close to home.
</p>

<p>
  For the redesign, I used colors that the shop already incorporates into its
  branding. I focused on the types of outdoor goods the store sells and the
  unique way Gearage gives used gear a second life through consignment.
</p>


<p>
  Most of the feedback I got was for the symbol quality and readability. 
  I have corrected that. There were also comments  about the words I chose,
  so I switched to outdoor instead of adventure. I did keep consignment just 
  because that idea is incredibly hard to translate into symbols or other words;
  hence why we have a dedicated word for the idea.
</p>
